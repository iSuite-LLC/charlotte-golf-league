## Sub Scores (Reserve Scores)

When you fill in for someone else, the match counts as a normal round **for the scheduled player only**. Your own score is banked as a **reserve score**:

- It never takes a round slot of its own — the Schedule and Results tabs keep showing the real matchup, unchanged.
- It **replaces your lowest counting round in your total points**, and only if it is strictly better.
- It changes **points only**. Your W-L-D record and avg NET are still computed from the rounds you played in your own slots.
- It is re-evaluated every time scores are refreshed, so if a worse round comes in later the reserve moves to that one instead.
